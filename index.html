I am a third-year PhD student in Philosophy at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Paolo Santorio.

I work on philosophy of language, epistemology, metaphysics, and philosophy of emotion.

I received my BA and MA in philosophy from East China Normal University. I grew up in Shanghai, China.

When I’m not doing philosophy, I enjoy watching films and listening to music. David Lynch and Kiyoshi Kurosawa are my favorite directors, and I’m a huge fan of NewJeans.
